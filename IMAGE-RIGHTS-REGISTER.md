# DG Property image-rights register

Last reviewed: 7 September 2026

This register records what can and cannot be established from the repository. It is not proof of ownership. Keep the relevant invoice, licence, photographer agreement, employee release, property-owner permission, or original-file record for every published image.

| Image group | Current repository finding | Required evidence / action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DG Property logo and brand marks | Used throughout the site. No separate trademark or design-rights evidence is stored in this repository. | Business owner to retain the original design files and designer assignment or licence. |
| Property listing images | Supplied through the DG Property listing/admin system. The code cannot establish who took or licensed each photo. | Require the uploader to confirm authority to publish, and retain the mandate or photographer/property-owner permission with the listing record. |
| Team and instructor portraits | Local and uploaded portraits are used. No model release or photographer licence was found in the repository. | Retain written permission from the person shown and the photographer/rightsholder. |
| News and success-story media | Uploaded through the admin system. No source or licence record is embedded in the repository. | Record creator, source, licence, consent date, and expiry/restrictions when each item is uploaded. |
| Template/property/course photographs in `public/assets/images` and `src/assets/images` | Source files are present, but no image-specific licence schedule was found. The repository's software licence does not prove rights to these photographs. | Replace any image whose provenance cannot be documented, or attach the applicable stock/commission licence to the business records. |
| Client/partner logos | The homepage logo strip is no longer rendered because client status and permission could not be verified from the repository. | Restore only logos for which DG Property has current written permission. |
| Social-share image | Generated for this project and uses the DG Property logo. | Retain the generation record and confirm the business has rights to the logo. |
| External Instagram feed | Removed from the rendered homepage, eliminating automatic third-party image loading and unclear reuse of feed media. | Link to the official Instagram account instead if needed; do not republish third-party posts without permission. |

## Publication checklist

- Record the image filename or listing/media ID, creator, source, rightsholder, permission or licence, permitted channels, and any expiry date.
- Obtain consent where an identifiable person appears and the use is not otherwise clearly authorised.
- Do not treat a web search result, social-media post, watermark removal, or possession of a file as publication permission.
- Route takedown requests to `info@dg-property.co.za` and temporarily unpublish disputed media while the evidence is checked.
